Owed

Who you owe, and who owes you.

Owed is a commitment tracker for iPhone and iPad. It keeps two ledgers, what you owe and what is owed to you. Capture a promise by typing, speaking, writing by hand, scanning a screenshot, or sharing a message into the app, and Owed fills in the person, the direction and the due date for you. It then nudges you in a free moment before something is due, and drafts a polite chaser when someone is late, in English or Arabic. Owed has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. Your commitments stay on your device unless you turn on sync with your own iCloud or Google Drive, and AI features are optional and use your own API key.

Highlights

  • Two ledgers: "I owe" and "Owed to me", grouped by Late, Today, This week and Later
  • Capture by text, voice, Apple Pencil, screenshot, share sheet or Siri
  • Nudges timed around your calendar and working week (Sunday to Thursday by default in the Gulf)
  • Follow-up drafts in Friendly, Firm or Formal tone, in English or Arabic
  • Notes and PDF annotation with Apple Pencil, organized in folders
  • Widgets, Siri and Shortcuts, and two-way sync with Apple Reminders
  • Optional: Google Tasks, Google Calendar free/busy, Telegram, webhooks, and your own Claude, ChatGPT or other AI key
