Owed Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

The short version
  • Owed has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive, store or see your commitments, notes or any other content.
  • Your data is stored on your device. It leaves your device only to services you choose and connect yourself: your own iCloud, your own Google account, an AI provider you set up with your own API key, your own Telegram bot, web addresses (webhooks) you enter, or an app you share to.
  • AI features are optional. Owed works fully without them. Before Owed sends anything to an AI provider, it tells you what will be sent and to whom, and asks for your permission.
  • You can delete everything and revoke every connection at any time. See Deleting your data.

1. Who we are

Owed ("Owed", "the app", "we", "us") is developed and published by Abdulaziz Al-Dubaib, an independent developer based in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. For any privacy question or request, email aldubaib@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Owed app for iPhone and iPad, including its widgets and its share extension, and this website.

2. Data the app handles on your device

To work, Owed stores the following on your iPhone or iPad. This is processing on your device. It is not sent to us.

  • Commitments: the title, the person involved, whether you owe it or it is owed to you, due date, urgency, notes, completion and postpone history, and the original text you captured.
  • Notes: notebooks, handwriting (Apple Pencil or finger) and PDFs you import or annotate.
  • Phone numbers you type for people, used only to open the right WhatsApp chat when you choose to send a follow-up.
  • Voice: when you hold the microphone button, Apple's speech recognition turns speech into text. Owed does not store audio. Recognition runs on the device when your language supports it; otherwise Apple's speech service processes it under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Photos and screenshots you pick or share: text is read on the device with Apple's text recognition. Owed reads only the images you choose.
  • Calendar (if you allow it): Owed reads the start and end times of busy events to time reminders in free gaps. It does not change your events and does not send them anywhere.
  • Reminders (if you turn on Reminders sync): Owed keeps an "Owed" list in Apple Reminders in sync with your commitments.
  • Settings and credentials: your preferences; AI API keys, Google sign-in tokens and your Telegram bot token. Keys and tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only. They are not synced or backed up and are never sent to us.

Commitments are understood on the device by Apple's on-device model (where available) or by Owed's built-in rules, unless you choose a cloud AI provider (see section 4.4).

3. What we collect

Nothing. The developer operates no server for Owed and receives no personal data from the app. Owed contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics through App Store Connect. That is controlled by Apple and your device settings. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed to help you.

4. When data leaves your device, and to whom

Data leaves your device only through features you turn on. In each case it goes directly from your device to the service you chose, under your own account or key. We never receive a copy. Each service handles your data under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to read. We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

4.1 iCloud

iCloud sync uses Apple's CloudKit private database and iCloud Drive under your Apple Account. The developer cannot access your private iCloud data. Apple handles it under Apple's Privacy Policy.

4.2 Google

Connecting Google is optional. Owed asks only for the permissions each feature needs (see section 5).

4.3 Microsoft 365

Microsoft 365 integration is not available in this version of Owed, and no data is sent to Microsoft. If it is added later, we will update this policy first.

4.4 Cloud AI providers

By default Owed uses on-device processing and sends nothing to any AI service. If you choose Claude, ChatGPT or another provider in Settings → AI engine and enter your own API key:

  • Owed shows you what will be sent and to which provider, and asks for your explicit permission before it sends anything. You can revoke that permission in Settings → Privacy.
  • What is sent: text you capture (typed, dictated, shared, forwarded to your Telegram bot, or given to Siri); images of handwriting when you ask Owed to read it; for "Ask Owed", the morning brief and the weekly review, a list of up to 80 of your open and recent commitments (title, person, due date and status); and the facts of a commitment when you draft a follow-up.
  • The request goes straight from your device to the provider with your key. We never see it. The provider processes and may keep it under your agreement with them and their privacy policy: Anthropic, OpenAI, or the service you entered.
  • We do not use your data to train any AI model.
  • To stop: switch back to "On-device", remove your key, and revoke permission. You can also delete the key in the provider's console.

4.5 Telegram

If you connect your own Telegram bot, Owed uses your bot token to fetch messages sent to that bot and to reply in your chat. Only the first chat that sends /start is accepted. Telegram handles these messages under the Telegram Privacy Policy.

4.6 Webhooks

If you add a webhook, Owed posts a short message about each change to the HTTPS address you entered. You control who receives it. Up to three webhooks can be set, and you can remove any of them at any time.

5. Google user data

This section explains how Owed accesses, uses, stores and shares data from your Google account. Owed requests these permissions only when you tap Connect, and you can connect Google Drive and Google Tasks/Calendar separately.

Sign-in tokens stay in this device's Keychain. Free/busy times are used in memory to schedule reminders and are not stored or sent anywhere else.
  • Use: only to provide the sync and reminder-timing features you can see in the app.

  • No AI training: Google user data is never used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

Owed's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To revoke Owed's access to Google, tap Disconnect in the app, then remove Owed at myaccount.google.com/connections. See section 7 to delete data Owed created in your Google account.

6. Retention

Your data stays on your device, and in any service you connected, until you delete it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to retain. Each connected service keeps data under its own retention rules.

7. Deleting your data and revoking access

  • Everything on the device: first remove your AI key and disconnect Google and Telegram in the app (this deletes the stored keys and tokens), then delete the Owed app. Deleting the app removes its database, notes stored on the device and settings. iOS may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted; they stay encrypted on that device only and are erased when you erase the device. To erase only commitments, use Settings → Data → Erase everything. Delete notes in the Notes tab.
  • iCloud: on iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Storage (Manage Account Storage) → Owed → Delete Data. Notes in the "Owed" folder of iCloud Drive can be deleted in the Files app.
  • Google Drive: at drive.google.com open Settings → Manage apps → Owed → Options → Delete hidden app data. Delete the "Owed Notes" folder like any other folder.
  • Google Tasks: delete the "Owed" list in Google Tasks.
  • Google access: Disconnect in Owed, then remove Owed at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • AI provider: Settings → AI engine → Remove (key), Settings → Privacy → Revoke permission, and delete the key in the provider's console. Ask the provider about data it holds.
  • Telegram: Disconnect Telegram in Owed. Delete the bot with @BotFather if you no longer need it.
  • Webhooks: swipe a webhook to remove it.
  • Apple Reminders: turn off Reminders sync and delete the "Owed" list in Reminders.
  • Device permissions: turn off Microphone, Speech Recognition, Calendars, Reminders or Photos for Owed in iOS Settings at any time.

8. Security

Owed connects to Apple, Google, Anthropic, OpenAI and Telegram over HTTPS, and webhooks must use HTTPS addresses. If you enter your own AI endpoint, use an HTTPS address. Google sign-in uses OAuth with PKCE in Apple's secure sign-in sheet; Owed never sees your Google password. Keys and tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain, readable only on this device after it has been unlocked once. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please protect your device with a passcode.

9. Children

Owed is a general productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Cloud AI providers have their own minimum ages; for example, their API terms generally require users to be adults.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, the EU and UK GDPR, and California privacy law (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because your data is on your device and in accounts you control, you can use these rights directly in the app and in those services, as described in section 7. We do not sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms. For questions or help, email aldubaib@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

11. International processing

The services you choose to connect (Apple, Google, AI providers, Telegram, webhook services) may process data in countries other than yours, under their own safeguards. The developer does not transfer your data anywhere.

12. Changes to this policy

If Owed starts handling data in a new way, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect, and we will note the change in the app's release notes. We will ask for your consent again where the law requires it.
