Owed Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

The short version
  • Owed has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer does not receive, store or see your commitments, notes or any other content.
  • Your data is stored on your device. It leaves your device only to services you choose and connect yourself: your own iCloud, your own Google account, an AI provider you set up with your own API key, your own Telegram bot, web addresses (webhooks) you enter, or an app you share to. Some of these exist only on iPhone and iPad. On Android the list is shorter; see section 14.
  • On Android, your data stays in the app's private storage on your device. The only thing that leaves is what you send to the AI provider you choose with your own key (an optional part of Owed Pro), after you give explicit permission. Purchases are handled by Google Play.
  • AI features are optional. Owed works fully without them. Before Owed sends anything to an AI provider, it tells you what will be sent and to whom, and asks for your permission.
  • You can delete everything and revoke every connection at any time. See Deleting your data.

1. Who we are

Owed ("Owed", "the app", "we", "us") is developed and published by Abdulaziz Al-Dubaib, an independent developer based in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia. For any privacy question or request, email aldubaib@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Owed app for iPhone and iPad, including its widgets and its share extension, the Owed app for Android, and this website. Sections 2, 4, 5, 7 and 8 describe iPhone and iPad. Section 14 describes Android.

2. Data the app handles on your device

To work, Owed stores the following on your iPhone or iPad. This is processing on your device. It is not sent to us.

  • Commitments: the title, the person involved, whether you owe it or it is owed to you, due date, urgency, notes, completion and postpone history, and the original text you captured.
  • Notes: notebooks, handwriting (Apple Pencil or finger) and PDFs you import or annotate.
  • Phone numbers you type for people, used only to open the right WhatsApp chat when you choose to send a follow-up.
  • Voice: when you hold the microphone button, Apple's speech recognition turns speech into text. Owed does not store audio. Recognition runs on the device when your language supports it; otherwise Apple's speech service processes it under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • Photos and screenshots you pick or share: text is read on the device with Apple's text recognition. Owed reads only the images you choose.
  • Calendar (if you allow it): Owed reads the start and end times of busy events to time reminders in free gaps. It does not change your events and does not send them anywhere.
  • Reminders (if you turn on Reminders sync): Owed keeps an "Owed" list in Apple Reminders in sync with your commitments.
  • Settings and credentials: your preferences; AI API keys, Google sign-in tokens and your Telegram bot token. Keys and tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only. They are not synced or backed up and are never sent to us.

Commitments are understood on the device by Apple's on-device model (where available) or by Owed's built-in rules, unless you choose a cloud AI provider (see section 4.4). Owed for Android handles data as described in section 14.

3. What we collect

Nothing. The developer operates no server for Owed and receives no personal data from the app. Owed contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, does not use the advertising identifier, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

If you choose to share diagnostics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us aggregated, anonymous usage and crash statistics through App Store Connect. That is controlled by Apple and your device settings. (For Android, see section 14.2.) If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply. We delete support emails when they are no longer needed to help you.

4. When data leaves your device, and to whom

Data leaves your device only through features you turn on. In each case it goes directly from your device to the service you chose, under your own account or key. We never receive a copy. Each service handles your data under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to read. We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not share it for advertising. 4.1 iCloud

iCloud sync uses Apple's CloudKit private database and iCloud Drive under your Apple Account. The developer cannot access your private iCloud data. Apple handles it under Apple's Privacy Policy.

4.2 Google

Connecting Google is optional. Owed asks only for the permissions each feature needs (see section 5).

4.3 Microsoft 365

Microsoft 365 integration is not available in this version of Owed, and no data is sent to Microsoft. If it is added later, we will update this policy first.

4.4 Cloud AI providers

By default Owed uses on-device processing and sends nothing to any AI service. If you choose Claude, ChatGPT or another provider in Settings → AI engine and enter your own API key:

  • Owed shows you what will be sent and to which provider, and asks for your explicit permission before it sends anything. You can revoke that permission in Settings → Privacy.
  • What is sent: text you capture (typed, dictated, shared, forwarded to your Telegram bot, or given to Siri); images of handwriting when you ask Owed to read it; for "Ask Owed", the morning brief and the weekly review, a list of up to 80 of your open and recent commitments (title, person, due date and status); and the facts of a commitment when you draft a follow-up.
  • The request goes straight from your device to the provider with your key. We never see it. The provider processes and may keep it under your agreement with them and their privacy policy: Anthropic, OpenAI, or the service you entered.
  • We do not use your data to train any AI model.
  • To stop: switch back to "On-device", remove your key, and revoke permission. You can also delete the key in the provider's console.

4.5 Telegram

If you connect your own Telegram bot, Owed uses your bot token to fetch messages sent to that bot and to reply in your chat. Only the first chat that sends /start is accepted. Telegram handles these messages under the Telegram Privacy Policy.

4.6 Webhooks

If you add a webhook, Owed posts a short message about each change to the HTTPS address you entered. You control who receives it. Up to three webhooks can be set, and you can remove any of them at any time.

6. Retention

Your data stays on your device, and in any service you connected, until you delete it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to retain. Each connected service keeps data under its own retention rules. On Android, backup files you export stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.

7. Deleting your data and revoking access

This section describes iPhone and iPad. For Android, see section 14.7.

  • Everything on the device: first remove your AI key and disconnect Google and Telegram in the app (this deletes the stored keys and tokens), then delete the Owed app. Deleting the app removes its database, notes stored on the device and settings. iOS may keep Keychain items after an app is deleted; they stay encrypted on that device only and are erased when you erase the device. To erase only commitments, use Settings → Data → Erase everything. Delete notes in the Notes tab.
  • iCloud: on iPhone or iPad, open Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Storage (Manage Account Storage) → Owed → Delete Data. Notes in the "Owed" folder of iCloud Drive can be deleted in the Files app.
  • Google Drive: at drive.google.com open Settings → Manage apps → Owed → Options → Delete hidden app data. Delete the "Owed Notes" folder like any other folder.
  • Google Tasks: delete the "Owed" list in Google Tasks.
  • Google access: Disconnect in Owed, then remove Owed at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • AI provider: Settings → AI engine → Remove (key), Settings → Privacy → Revoke permission, and delete the key in the provider's console. Ask the provider about data it holds.
  • Telegram: Disconnect Telegram in Owed. Delete the bot with @BotFather if you no longer need it.
  • Webhooks: swipe a webhook to remove it.
  • Apple Reminders: turn off Reminders sync and delete the "Owed" list in Reminders.
  • Device permissions: turn off Microphone, Speech Recognition, Calendars, Reminders or Photos for Owed in iOS Settings at any time.

8. Security

This section describes iPhone and iPad. For Android, see section 14.8. Owed connects to Apple, Google, Anthropic, OpenAI and Telegram over HTTPS, and webhooks must use HTTPS addresses. If you enter your own AI endpoint, use an HTTPS address. Google sign-in uses OAuth with PKCE in Apple's secure sign-in sheet; Owed never sees your Google password. Keys and tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain, readable only on this device after it has been unlocked once. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please protect your device with a passcode.

9. Children

Owed is a general productivity app and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Cloud AI providers have their own minimum ages; for example, their API terms generally require users to be adults. On Google Play, Owed is intended for users aged 13 and older.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as the Saudi Personal Data Protection Law, the EU and UK GDPR, and California privacy law (CCPA/CPRA) give you rights to access, correct, delete or port your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because your data is on your device and in accounts you control, you can use these rights directly in the app and in those services, as described in section 7. We do not sell or "share" personal information as California law defines those terms. For questions or help, email aldubaib@gmail.com. You may also contact your local data protection authority.

11. International processing

The services you choose to connect (Apple, Google, AI providers, Telegram, webhook services) may process data in countries other than yours, under their own safeguards. The developer does not transfer your data anywhere.

12. Changes to this policy

If Owed starts handling data in a new way, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect, and we will note the change in the app's release notes. We will ask for your consent again where the law requires it.

14. Owed on Android

This section applies to the Owed app for Android. It is part of this policy. Where it differs from an earlier section, this section applies to Android. The first version of Owed for Android does not include iCloud, Siri, widgets, Apple Reminders, Google Drive, Google Tasks or Google Calendar connections, Telegram, webhooks, handwriting or the Notes tab, so the parts of this policy about those features do not apply to Android. If any of them is added later, we will update this policy first.

14.1 Data the app handles on your Android device

  • Commitments: the title, the person involved, whether you owe it or it is owed to you, due date, urgency, notes, completion and postpone history, and the original text you captured. They are stored in the app's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read under normal Android rules.
  • Settings and credentials: your preferences and, if you use Cloud AI, your own AI API key and your recorded permission. The API key is stored encrypted using the Android Keystore. It is never sent to us.
  • Backup files you create: you can export your data as a JSON file to a location you choose, and import such a file later. The file is yours. Owed does not upload it anywhere.

Owed turns off Android's automatic app backup, so your commitments and keys are not copied to a cloud backup. To move to a new phone, use the export and import feature.

14.2 What we collect on Android

Nothing. There are no accounts, and the developer operates no server for Owed. Owed for Android contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software and does not use the advertising identifier. If you choose to share usage and diagnostics data in your Android settings, Google may give developers aggregated, anonymous statistics through Google Play Console. That is controlled by Google and your device settings. If you email us, we receive your email address and message and use them only to reply.

14.3 Voice dictation

When you tap the microphone button, Owed opens Android's built-in speech recognition screen. Owed itself never records or receives audio and does not ask for the microphone permission. It receives only the text that the speech recognizer returns. The recognizer is a system service. On most Android devices it is provided by Google, and it may process your speech on the device or send it to its servers, under its own privacy policy (for Google, the Google Privacy Policy). Owed asks the recognizer to prefer on-device recognition where your device supports it, but the recognizer decides. If your device has no speech recognition service, Owed hides the microphone button.

14.4 Cloud AI (Owed Pro)

Cloud AI is optional and is part of Owed Pro. Without it, Owed sends nothing to any AI service. If you turn it on and enter your own API key:

  • Owed shows you what will be sent and to which provider, and asks for your explicit permission before it sends anything.
  • What is sent: the text you enter or capture, and the commitment data needed for the feature you use, such as Ask Owed and the Weekly Review.
  • Where it goes: directly from your device to the provider you chose, using your own key: Anthropic (privacy policy), OpenAI (privacy policy), or an OpenAI-compatible service at an HTTPS address you enter. Owed does not accept plain http addresses. We never see these requests, and there is no developer server in between.
  • The provider processes and may keep the data under your agreement with them and their privacy policy. We do not use your data to train any AI model.
  • To stop: remove your key or turn Cloud AI off in Owed's Settings. Settings → Erase everything also deletes saved keys and your recorded permission. You can also delete the key in the provider's console.

14.5 Purchases

Owed Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The app never sees your payment details, and we do not receive them.

14.6 Notifications

Reminders and nudges are local notifications that Owed schedules on your device. No server or push service is involved. You can turn them off for Owed in Android Settings → Apps → Owed → Notifications.

14.7 Deleting your data on Android

  • Inside Owed: Settings → Erase everything deletes your commitments, preferences, saved AI keys and AI permission from the device. It does not remove your Owed Pro purchase, which Google Play keeps for your Google account.
  • Uninstalling Owed deletes all of the app's data from the device. You can also clear it in Android Settings → Apps → Owed → Storage.
  • Backup files you exported stay where you saved them until you delete them yourself.
  • AI provider: delete your key in the provider's console, and ask the provider about data it holds.
  • Voice: data handled by the system speech recognizer is governed by that service's own settings and policy.

14.8 Security on Android

Owed for Android uses HTTPS only for network connections and does not allow unencrypted connections. Your API key is stored encrypted with the Android Keystore, and your data lives in the app's private storage. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so please protect your phone with a screen lock.
